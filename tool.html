    What makes this different from eBird's alerts?

    eBird alerts are a firehose — every rare bird in your region. Holdout is the opposite: a quiet list of just your nemeses, written in the language of confession. The AI essay treats missing the bird as a story, not a data gap.

    Is my saga private?

    Your essay is private to you. Recent post-mortems above show only the species, never your text or location. We'll never share your saga without explicit consent.

    How is the essay generated?

    Gemini 2.0 Flash, with a prompt tuned to write in a quiet, observational register. We feed it your saga and the species' known behavior; it returns prose that's part field guide, part journal entry.
